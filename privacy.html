Privacy policy

Lumen collects nothing.

Effective September 25, 2026.

In short: Lumen doesn't collect, store, share or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

Your projects and media

Maps you create, and any photos or videos you add to them, are saved only on your device, inside Lumen's own storage. They are never uploaded anywhere.

Photos and videos

When you add media, Lumen uses the system photo picker. You choose exactly which items to share, and Lumen never gets access to the rest of your library.

Purchases

Lumen Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; Lumen never receives your name, email or payment details. The app only asks the App Store whether Pro is unlocked on your account.

Projectors and displays

Lumen sends images to a connected display (HDMI or AirPlay) using iOS's standard external-display features. It doesn't scan your network, and the only server it talks to is Apple's App Store, to check your Pro purchase.

Children

Lumen collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.
