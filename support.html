How do I connect a projector?

Use a USB-C or Lightning to HDMI adapter, or open Control Center, tap Screen Mirroring and choose an Apple TV or AirPlay-compatible projector. Lumen fills the projector with your map automatically.

Do I need a projector to use Lumen?

No. You can build and save maps without one; the preview in the app matches the output exactly. Your map appears as soon as you connect.

How do I line up a shape with a real object?

On the Map tab, drag each corner in the preview. The projector shows outlines and a crosshair on the active corner, so you can watch the wall while you adjust. For precise moves, pick a corner in the small diagram, then drag the precision pad or tap the arrows to nudge one pixel at a time.

The picture looks cut off or has black borders.

Check the Fit setting on the Content tab: Fill crops to cover the shape, Fit shows the whole image, and Stretch distorts it to the exact shape. Also turn off any zoom or keystone correction on the projector itself, and let Lumen do the warping.

What does Lumen Pro include?

Free Lumen maps up to 3 surfaces per map and keeps 3 saved maps, with photos, text, 7 patterns, the Pulse and Hue effects, test patterns and picture controls. Lumen Pro adds unlimited surfaces and maps, mesh warp, masks, camera mapping, scenes and shows, sound-reactive light, video, live camera, new generators, stacked effects and blend modes, remotes (iPhone, Apple Watch, MIDI, clickers), kiosk mode, iCloud sync, edge blending and themed templates.

How much is Pro, and how do I cancel?

$2.99 a month, $14.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, or $29.99 once for lifetime access. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel, at least 24 hours before the period ends, in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions (or Lumen › Settings › Manage subscription). Lifetime is a single payment with no renewals.

How do I restore Lumen Pro on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Lumen, tap "Lumen Pro" in the library, then tap "Restore purchase". Family Sharing members get Pro automatically.

How do I redeem a promo code?

Tap "Lumen Pro" in the library (or any feature marked PRO), then "Redeem code". Enter the code in Apple's sheet and Pro unlocks right away. You can also redeem codes in the App Store app under your account.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where are my projects stored?

On your device only. Lumen has no account and no cloud sync. Deleting the app deletes its projects.
