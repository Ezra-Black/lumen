How it works

Your first show in three steps.

No account, no setup, no learning curve. If you can drag a corner, you can projection map.

01

Connect

Plug in a USB-C or Lightning HDMI adapter, or use AirPlay. Lumen fills the projector automatically.

02

Map

Drag corners onto the object. The projector shows guides on the wall, and everything snaps into place.

03

Fill

Pick a photo, a video or a built-in pattern, then add a live effect. You're on.

Precision

Line it up by eye.

Your phone is the remote. While you map, the projector draws outlines and a crosshair on the corner you're moving, so you watch the wall, not the screen.

  • Corners snap to each other, so box faces meet cleanly
  • A precision pad with fine and coarse gain
  • 1-pixel nudges, and your finger never covers the corner
Effects

Six live effects.

Pulse, Hue, Drift, Kaleido, Ripple and Split, each with amount and speed. They run live on the GPU, so what you see on your phone is exactly what hits the wall.

  • One tap to try each look
  • Different effects on every surface
  • Smooth at full projector resolution
Content

Your photos. Your videos.

Pull anything from your library and it wraps onto the surface with correct perspective. Videos loop silently, and built-in patterns come in any colours you like.

  • Fill, Fit or Stretch
  • Soft edges and opacity for each surface
  • An alignment grid, plasma, rings, stripes and more
Scale

One shape or a whole stage.

Add as many surfaces as you need, each with its own content and effect. Start from a template for a single wall, a box or three panels.

  • Blackout turns the projector dark with one tap
  • Your screen stays awake during a show
  • Everything saves on your device

FAQ

Good questions.

Do I need a projector to try it?

No. You can build and save maps without one, and the preview in the app matches the output exactly. Your map appears the moment you connect.

Which projectors work?

Any projector with an HDMI input, using a USB-C or Lightning to HDMI adapter, plus Apple TV or AirPlay-compatible projectors through Screen Mirroring.

Is it really free?

Yes. No subscription, no ads, no watermark, and no effect packs to buy. Every feature is included.

What happens to my photos and data?

Nothing leaves your device. Lumen has no account and no analytics, and it collects no data. You choose media with the system photo picker, so it never sees the rest of your library. See the privacy policy.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes. Lumen runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 18 or later, and the bigger canvas on iPad is lovely for precise mapping.

Can I map curved surfaces?

Lumen maps four-corner surfaces, which covers walls, boxes, frames, screens and most stage pieces. You can combine as many surfaces as you need for more complex objects.
